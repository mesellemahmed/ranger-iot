redistribution permissions, and does not replace a LICENSE file.

Before public distribution, the project rights holder must:

1. Determine the intended license for original source code.
2. Confirm permission to publish contributed or third-party code.
3. Review bundled dependencies and their respective licenses.
4. Confirm attribution and redistribution obligations for
   Contiki-NG and other third-party components.
5. Determine whether datasets and scientific evidence artifacts

Until these decisions are resolved, public release readiness
remains blocked by licensing review.
